# Dragon Rampage — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 3, 2026
**Developer:** Chris Gaines
**Contact:** chrisgaines47@gmail.com

Dragon Rampage is a single-player game for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what the app does — and does not do — with your information. The short version: **Dragon Rampage does not collect, store, or share any personal data.**

## What the app collects

Nothing. Dragon Rampage has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics or tracking software, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The app does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or any other personal information on your device.

## Game progress

Your campaign progress, stars, best scores, and settings are saved **only on your device**, using the standard local storage that iOS provides to apps. This data never leaves your device through the app, and it is deleted when you delete the app. It may be included in your device's iCloud or iTunes backup according to your own device settings, which are controlled by Apple and not by Dragon Rampage.

## In-app purchases

Dragon Rampage offers one optional in-app purchase, "Full Game", which unlocks additional content. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The app never sees your payment details, Apple ID, or billing information. When you buy or restore the purchase, Apple tells the app only whether the purchase is owned so the content can be unlocked.

Apple's handling of purchase data is described in [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and the [App Store & Privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/app-store/) notice.

## Ratings and reviews

After you complete certain levels, the app may ask iOS to show Apple's standard "rate this app" prompt. That prompt is provided and controlled by Apple; the app receives no information about whether you responded or what you chose.

## Third parties

The app is built with the Unity game engine and uses Unity's In-App Purchasing package to talk to the App Store. Dragon Rampage does not enable Unity Analytics, Unity Ads, or any other Unity service that collects user data. No data is sent to the developer or to any third party by the app.

## Children

Dragon Rampage does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the app collects no data at all, there is nothing to review, correct, or delete.

## Your choices

Because the app collects nothing, there are no data settings to manage inside it. To remove all locally saved game progress, delete the app from your device.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of Dragon Rampage ever changes how it handles data, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

## Support

For questions about this policy or help with the game, email **chrisgaines47@gmail.com**. This page also serves as the support page for Dragon Rampage.
